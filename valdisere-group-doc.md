## Snow outlook

| | Top of ski area | Bottom |
|---|---|---|
| Historic **November** average depth | 36 cm | 7 cm |
| Historic **December** average depth | 106 cm | 54 cm |

**Forecast:** strong El Niño — a mild, slow start to the European snow season, but
above-normal snowfall **above 1800–2000 m in the western Alps**. Val d'Isère village sits
at 1850 m and the ski area runs to ~3450 m, so we're on the right side of that line.

**Backups if the lower slopes are thin** — all already covered by the package:

- Our pass includes **Tignes**, which opens earlier — **Grande Motte glacier, 3456 m**
- **Pissaillas glacier** on the Val d'Isère side
- Europe's largest snowmaking plant: ~900 cannons over 65 km of piste, able to cover the
  Bellevarde face in under a week

**Expect:** good skiing top and mid-mountain on firm machine-made and glacier snow; lower
runs and the ski home to the village a coin flip; off-piste probably not in.
